Summary of Pre Imperial China

The Xia Dynasty is the first dynasty in traditional historiography, while some scholars, Beginning with the doubting Antiquity school lead by Gu Jiegang and especially those of western historians and Sinologists, considered it to be Unsubstantiated or legendary due to lack of written evidence that confirms that Erlitou is Xia. While the majority of Chinese historians consider it to be historical, as they identify with Erlitou culture (c. 3500-2600 ;1870 -1300 BC ) to the same period and location which the Xia was dated by Xia–Shang–Zhou Chronology Project (c. 2070 – c. 1600 BC ) , although other dates have been proposed. Alternative dates include calculation by Liu Xiu (2205- 1766 BC), and Pankenier supported by Nivison. According to Sima Qian`s “Record of grand historians” it was first founded by Yu the Great after the Legendary period Known “Five Sovereign and three emperors” whose success in stopping the Yellow river flooding led to consolidation of the tribe. Finally, with his forces he managed to suppress the Sanmiao tribe and due to his effort and work the final emperor of the previous period Shun relinquished his throne to Yu. The Xia would eventually be deposed as King Jie who is said to be immoral and tyrannical according to accounts. This led to the battle of Mingtao which the Shang, a vassal of Xia, would defeat the weakened Xia paving the way for the overthrow of Xia and the rise of Zi lu or Tang who will create the new Shang Dynasty.

The Shang dynasty is the second dynasty in traditional historiography. It is known for their divination with usage of Turtle shell, highly sophisticated pottery and bronze casting which is used in their ritual sacrifice and their downfall which is immortalised in a Ming era epic called “Investiture of the Gods” According to Sima Qian, they are founded first by an ancestor named Xie who was birthed by Jiadi, the second wife of Emperor ku due to swallowing an egg. Eventually he would become fief of Shang after helping Yu the Great in flood control. Eventually his descended Tang would overthrow the Xia Dynasty. From there a common convention within the historiography of the Shang is to divide the dynasty to Early and late period with the division date being Pan Geng who is traditionally said to have moved the capital to Anyang. Although more recent convection is to split into third with Middle Shang being a Transition period between the former and latter period. With the later period of ANyang, the site of Yinxu in particular has been a treasure trove of artifacts with the tomb of Fu Hao being one of the only tomb during the Shang period that is well preserved and unplundered . Outside of these two there are other archaeological finds including Oracle bones, Bronze sacrificial vessel and Erlitou archeological site and the traditional account of the Shang and Zhou such as Book of document, Bamboo annals and Shiji

Eastern Zhou period, commonly split up into two eras, the first half which is the Spring and Autumn period (named after the Spring and Autumn Annals) and second half the warring State period. During this period royal power waned and collapse with the vassals grew in more importance and power over the now figurehead King. This would reach a climax when Jin, a vassal of the Zhou, would be partitioned by three rival families heralding the Warring state period. As the Zhou is now reduced to a minor power, the new powerful lords declare themselves as kings as the numerous bickering rises and falls, eventually solidifying into Seven states. However, while constant warfare occurred during this period was the golden period of Chinese philosophy called "the hundred School of thought" while some of the Philosophy would fade away in time others such as Legalism, Confucianism and Taoism would prove pivotal in the later chinese thoughts and Philosophy.

While many states would rise and fall, as alliance waxed and wane .It would be the Qin that would reunify the nation.
