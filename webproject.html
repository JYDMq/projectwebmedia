Summary of Pre Imperial China

The Xia Dynasty is the first dynasty in traditional historiography, while some scholars, Beginning with the doubting Antiquity school lead by Gu Jiegang and especially those of western historians and Sinologists, considered it to be Unsubstantiated or legendary due to lack of written evidence that confirms that Erlitou is Xia. While the majority of Chinese historians consider it to be historical, as they identify with Erlitou culture (c. 3500-2600 ;1870 -1300 BC ) to the same period and location which the Xia was dated by Xia–Shang–Zhou Chronology Project (c. 2070 – c. 1600 BC ) , although other dates have been proposed. Alternative dates include calculation by Liu Xiu (2205- 1766 BC), and Pankenier supported by Nivison. According to Sima Qian`s “Record of grand historians” it was first founded by Yu the Great after the Legendary period Known “Five Sovereign and three emperors” whose success in stopping the Yellow river flooding led to consolidation of the tribe. Finally, with his forces he managed to suppress the Sanmiao tribe and due to his effort and work the final emperor of the previous period Shun relinquished his throne to Yu. The Xia would eventually be deposed as King Jie who is said to be immoral and tyrannical according to accounts. This led to the battle of Mingtao which the Shang, a vassal of Xia, would defeat the weakened Xia paving the way for the overthrow of Xia and the rise of Zi lu or Tang who will create the new Shang Dynasty.
